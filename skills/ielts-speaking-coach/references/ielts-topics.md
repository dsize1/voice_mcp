# 雅思口语题库与 7 分参考答案

> 用途：出题、给示范。答案**不要照背**——先让用户自己说，再给参考版本对比。
> 所有答案按"7 分特征"写：有明确观点、具体细节、自然连接词、长短句混合、至少一处从句。

## Part 1（4–5 分钟，日常话题，每问 2–3 句）

**Hometown / Work / Study**
- Where is your hometown? — `It's a mid-sized city in the south, about two hours from the coast. It's not a tourist destination, which is actually why I like it.`
- Do you work or study? — `I work as a software engineer, mostly on backend systems. It's demanding, but I get to solve new problems every week, which keeps me interested.`

**Home / Accommodation**
- Do you live in a house or an apartment? — `I live in a flat on the eighth floor. The best part is the view — you can see the river from the balcony.`

**Free time / Hobbies**
- What do you do in your free time? — `I've been learning to cook recently. I'm not good at it yet, but I find chopping vegetables weirdly relaxing.`

**Technology**
- How often do you use your phone? — `Probably more than I should. I use it for work, so it's hard to draw a line between being productive and just scrolling.`

## Part 2（1 分钟准备 + 说 1–2 分钟）

### 1. Describe a person who has influenced you.
> I'd like to talk about my first English teacher, Ms. Chen. She taught me when I was about twelve, and honestly, most of what I know about learning a language came from her.
> She had this habit of never correcting us in front of the whole class. Instead, she'd write the mistake on a small card and hand it to us after the lesson. It sounds like a small thing, but it meant we weren't afraid to speak up.
> What impressed me most was her patience with the same question. I once asked her the difference between "think" and "sink" four times in one week, and she explained it four different ways without ever sighing.
> Looking back, I think what she really taught me wasn't vocabulary — it was that being wrong is just part of the process. I still remind myself of that whenever I'm learning something new.

### 2. Describe a place you like to relax in.
> The place I go to when I need to switch off is a small café about ten minutes from my flat. It's nothing special — a few wooden tables, a cat that sleeps on the windowsill — but that's exactly the point.
> I usually go there on Sunday mornings with a book. Because it's quiet, I can actually read for an hour without checking my phone. When the weather's good, I sit outside and watch the street wake up.
> What makes it work for me is that nobody there expects anything from me. It's not a place where I meet people or get work done; it's just somewhere I can sit and think.
> I've been going there for about two years now, and honestly, I'd rather lose a Saturday afternoon there than spend it scrolling.

### 3. Describe a skill you learned that was difficult.
> I'd like to talk about learning to drive, which took me far longer than I expected.
> The theory part was fine — I passed the written test first time. But the practical side was a different story. I failed the road test twice, both times on the same thing: I hesitated at junctions, which the examiner read as uncertainty.
> What finally helped was practising with a friend rather than an instructor. With an instructor I was performing; with a friend I could make mistakes and laugh about them, and my confidence came back.
> I passed on the third attempt. It sounds trivial, but it taught me that the obstacle wasn't the skill itself — it was the fear of being watched while doing it badly.

## Part 3（4–5 分钟，抽象讨论）

### 关于学习
- **Why do some people learn languages faster than others?**
  `I don't think it's mainly talent. The people who improve quickly are usually the ones who are comfortable sounding foolish. They speak early, they get corrected, and they don't take it personally.`
- **Is it better to learn a language in a classroom or in the country?**
  `Ideally both, but I'd prioritise immersion. A classroom gives you structure, but real input — overheard conversations, small misunderstandings — is what makes the language stick.`
- **Should schools teach more practical skills?**
  `I'd say yes, but I'd be careful about it. Financial literacy, for instance, is something almost everyone needs and almost nobody is taught well. The risk is that "practical" can become an excuse to cut the arts, which teach a different kind of thinking.`

### 关于科技
- **How has technology changed the way people communicate?**
  `On balance it's made us faster but shallower. I can reach a friend in another country instantly, yet we tend to exchange short messages instead of having a real conversation. The medium shapes the message.`
- **Do you think AI will replace language teachers?**
  `Not replace, but reshape. An AI can correct your pronunciation at 2 a.m. and never get tired, which is genuinely useful. What it can't do is make you want to keep going — that's still a human job.`

### 关于社会
- **Is it important for people to keep traditions?**
  `I think traditions matter when they're alive rather than preserved. A festival that people actually look forward to is valuable; one that's performed for tourists is more like a museum piece.`

## 出题原则（给教练）

1. **Part 1 从用户档案的话题池挑**，避免连续两次同话题。
2. **Part 2 必须带 4 个小问**（人物/地点/物品/经历/事件），并在 1 分钟后提醒"还剩 1 分钟"。
3. **Part 3 的问题要与 Part 2 有主题衔接**（Part 2 说老师 → Part 3 谈教育）。
4. 参考答案里**故意保留一处口语化表达**（`weirdly relaxing`、`it sounds trivial, but…`），
   因为 7 分需要 natural idiomatic language，不是书面范文。
5. 用户要求"更难的题"时，优先加深 Part 3 的抽象度，而不是换生僻话题。

## 常见扣分点（提醒用户，不要一次全说）

| 扣分点 | 具体表现 | 改法 |
|---|---|---|
| 背模板 | "In contemporary society, people are increasingly aware…" | 用具体个人例子开头 |
| 答案太短 | Part 1 只答 2 个词 | 加"为什么/例子"：观点 + 原因 + 小例子 |
| 没展开 | Part 2 说 40 秒就停 | 准备时写 4 个关键词，每个讲 2–3 句 |
| 卡住就沉默 | 超过 2 秒空白 | 教 fill-in 句式：`That's an interesting question…`、`I've never thought about it, but…` |
| 全是简单句 | 每句都 I + 动词 + 宾语 | 加一处从句、一处非谓语 |
| 发音平 | 全句同一音高 | 句末降调 + 每句至少一个重音 |
